Bazaltoli Turf & Café

Operating costs

Billiards and gaming income assumes 2 pool tables at about ₹200 per table-hour for 5 hours a day, plus console and table-game sales. Staff assumed: manager, 2 ground attendants, games-hall attendant, cook, 2 café helpers, security guard. Other fixed costs cover DG fuel, turf grooming and infill top-up, marketing, insurance, booking software and admin. Repairs are taken at 1.5% of capital cost. Year 1 runs at 75% of steady-state revenue and year 2 at 90%; revenue and costs rise 5% a year from year 4. Tax is taken at a flat 25% of profit for illustration.

Before you meet the bank

The sketch map's NOC was issued for land sale and was valid only up to 6.7.2014. For a commercial building on this plot you will need fresh approvals in the borrower's name. The land is recorded under a periodic patta, so confirm with the Circle Office whether conversion from agricultural to commercial use is required before TDA will sanction a building plan.
